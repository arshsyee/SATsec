# Go-To-Market Plan

**Launch Target:** July 31, 2026
**Goal:** 50 paid subscribers by launch day

---

## Target Customers

### Primary: Freelance Web Developers & Small Agencies
- Build 5-20 sites/year for clients
- Need to prove quality of work — a score report is a deliverable
- Pain: manually checking Lighthouse, GTMetrix, WAVE separately
- SATsec gives them one score to show clients

### Secondary: Marketing Teams at SMBs
- Manage company website, care about SEO rankings
- Get burned by security issues (hacked sites, SSL expiry)
- Pain: don't know what's wrong until traffic drops
- SATsec gives them proactive alerts

### Later: DevOps / Platform Engineering Teams
- Want to integrate audits into CI/CD pipeline
- Blocked until API keys ship (Phase 3)

---

## Channels

### Week 1-4: Build an Audience
- **Twitter/X**: Post daily audit results for well-known sites (educational content)
  - "We audited [popular site] — here's what they got wrong"
  - No need to ask permission — auditing public URLs is legal
- **Reddit**: r/webdev, r/SEO, r/smallbusiness — answer questions, mention SATsec naturally
- **LinkedIn**: Target "web developer", "digital agency owner" — post accessibility/SEO tips

### Week 5-8: Beta Program
- Recruit 20 beta users from above channels
- Offer 3 months free Pro in exchange for weekly feedback
- Fix the top 3 issues they report before launch

### Week 9-10: Launch
- **Product Hunt**: Schedule for Tuesday or Wednesday (highest traffic days)
  - Need 5-10 upvote supporters lined up in advance
  - Write a compelling tagline: "Website health monitoring that actually tells you what to fix"
- **Hacker News Show HN**: Post same week as Product Hunt
  - HN loves tools with real technical depth — lean into the 61 audit checks angle
- **Email**: Send launch announcement to everyone who signed up during beta

---

## Positioning

**vs. Google Lighthouse:** Lighthouse is a one-time browser check. SATsec is continuous monitoring with alerts. You don't have to remember to run it.

**vs. Semrush / Ahrefs:** Those are $100-500/month SEO suites. SATsec is $29/month focused on actionable health scores across 4 dimensions, not keyword research.

**vs. GTMetrix:** Performance only. SATsec also covers security and accessibility (legal liability).

**Headline:** "Monitor your website's health across Performance, SEO, Accessibility, and Security — get alerted the moment something breaks."

---

## Pricing Psychology
- Free tier must be genuinely useful (5 audits/day) — drives word of mouth
- Pro at $29 is under the "ask my boss" threshold for freelancers
- Business at $99 is a rounding error for agencies billing $5k+/month per client
- Annual pricing: 2 months free (Pro $290/yr, Business $990/yr) — improves cash flow
